Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy describes the PULSSIX Media informational website and our planned use of authorized social-platform APIs.

Information collected by this website

This static website does not have account registration, contact forms, or a PULSSIX-operated analytics tracker. We do not ask visitors to provide personal information here. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request data under its own policies. Follow GitHub's privacy notice for more information.

Social media and APIs

PULSSIX plans to use the Pinterest and X APIs to manage content on our own authorized accounts and review available performance statistics (for example, impressions, saves, and engagement counts). We do not request access to other people's private Pinterest boards or messages. Any use of platform data will be subject to the permissions granted and the applicable platform policies. Features not yet activated do not collect platform analytics.

How information is used

Available authorized account information and aggregate content-performance metrics, when connected, are intended for scheduling, publishing, editorial quality checks, performance analysis, and preventing duplicate content. We do not sell personal information or platform data.

Data sharing and storage

Content workflow records and authorized account credentials, when configured, are intended to be stored in access-controlled operational systems rather than in this public website or its GitHub repository. Limited service providers may process data when needed to host the website or run authorized integrations, under their respective terms.

Retention and security

Operational records should be retained only while needed for publication, accounting, security, and quality review. We use reasonable access controls, but no online system can guarantee complete security.

Links to other sites

Our content may link to Pinterest, X, GitHub and other external websites. Their separate privacy policies apply to visits and interactions there.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests relating to PULSSIX, contact the project owner through the PULSSIX GitHub repository. Please do not post personal or confidential information in public GitHub issues.

Changes

We may revise this notice as the service evolves. The effective date above will be updated when we make changes.
